description: I was a neurotechnology systems intern with the <a href=https://www.neuropiano.org/>Music Excellence Project</a> of Sony CSL, Tokyo, Japan. I developed a real-time multimodal system for music preference decoding, combining neurophysiology and auditory signal processing with linear and deep learning approaches. My development of the system improved decoding accuracies by 15% over previous approaches, and enabled real-time feedback for music recommendation with latencies ~80 ms. This system was presented as an extended abstract and late breaking demo of the <a href=https://ismir2025.ismir.net/>26th ISMIR Conference</a>.
