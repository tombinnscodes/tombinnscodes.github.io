I'm a freelance software engineer and research scientist from the UK.

I am finishing my PhD in Computational Neuroscience, with a focus on signal processing, machine learning, human-computer interfaces, computational modelling, and statistics.

Prior to this, I graduated with a Master's degree in Neuroscience, focusing on signal processing, machine learning, and brain-computer interfaces.

I have also worked in industry as a Neurotechnology Systems intern at Sony Computer Science Labs, Tokyo, building real-time multimodal systems for neural decoding.

Through this, I have over 6 years of experience coding in Python and MATLAB, including contributions to open-source projects used by thousands of scientists worldwide.
